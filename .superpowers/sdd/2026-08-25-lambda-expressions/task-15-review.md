#### Important (Should Fix)

1. `scripts/capture-lambda-comparison.ts:172-183`, `scripts/capture-lambda-comparison.ts:206-235`, `scripts/capture-lambda-comparison.ts:753-774`, `tests/visual/lambda-reference.test.ts:225-278` — the manifest never retains the measured stroke endpoints, junction identities, or normalized trajectories, and the test never compares application geometry with reference geometry. `maxTargetError` compares each mode only with its own final frame and silently skips strokes with no match; `summarize` then discards the raw geometry. Cross-mode checks compare only four booleans/counts. Consequently an application with the correct phase labels but wrong junction destinations, an unmoved persistent junction, missing unmatched strokes, a discontinuous snap between samples, or a different docking trajectory can still pass. This misses the central requirements at `task-15-brief.md:52-60`. Retain normalized, correspondence-addressable stroke/junction observations in the manifest and compare reference-derived topology, persistent-junction displacement/destinations, copy shape/parking/docking, and inter-sample continuity against both application render paths with explicit tolerances.

2. `scripts/capture-lambda-comparison.ts:206-235`, `tests/visual/lambda-reference.test.ts:281-305`, `tests/visual/lambda-reference.test.ts:321-326` — only copy colors are serialized and asserted. Redex and argument lineage colors are reduced to counts/spans, and no test checks their `#f06aa7`/`#f0bd55` ramps or neutralization back to the renderer's base color. The endpoint base-color raster assertions can be satisfied by any base-colored incident wire elsewhere on the canvas. Thus the report's claim at `task-15-report.md:51-52` is not backed by the manifest consumer, and incorrect redex/argument tracking or premature neutralization would pass despite `task-15-brief.md:61-65`. Serialize colors by lineage at every sample and require the reference-derived identify/copy/settle progression, with Lambda-specific raster evidence rather than whole-canvas palette presence.

3. `scripts/capture-lambda-comparison.ts:363-390`, `scripts/capture-lambda-comparison.ts:457-478`, `tests/visual/lambda-reference.test.ts:225-305` — the 3D image is rendered from `sceneAt`, but its `structural` evidence is produced by a separate `sampleBetaMotion` call. `threeMetrics` measures plane/branch geometry and aggregate pixels only; it does not reconstruct copy membership, stroke completeness, lineage topology, or junction destinations from the `presented` Lambda entities. A faulty 3D embedding that drops most of a copy, misassigns a lineage, or maps its strokes to the wrong destination can therefore pass using the correct unembedded motion frame. Derive 3D structural/color observations from the actual `presented.entities` used by WebGL (including source stroke/copy identity and connections), and compare those observations with the reference/application plan instead of reusing the 2D motion sample as the 3D witness.
